Our Timeline

Four Eras, One Vision

1980

The Founding in Hong Kong

In 1980, the three founding Mok brothers established a factory in Hong Kong, launching an industrial trading business centred on artificial plants. By actively introducing advanced production technology, they laid the foundation of Wah Tat Industrial.

1980 Factory founded in Hong Kong The Mok Brothers
1984 – 2000

Entering Mainland China, Expanding Markets

Seizing the opportunities of China's reform and opening-up, the Mok brothers became early pioneers in the mainland market. A new factory was established in Jiangmen, Guangdong Province, where the company developed premium products that earned widespread acclaim.

1984 New factory in Jiangmen Premium product development
2000 – 2009

New Frontiers, New Talent

To embrace the opportunities and challenges of the new century, the company invested heavily in the real estate market and expanded into diversified businesses. Premium properties were acquired across Hong Kong, Shenzhen, Jiangmen and Guangzhou, while the purchase and revitalisation of industrial buildings and shops generated steady rental income. In 2006, Wah Kei Real Estate was established, marking the company's entry into property development.

2000 Real estate investment 2006 Wah Kei Real Estate founded
2009 – Present

Diversification & Group Formation

From 2009 onward, the company invested in diverse businesses across Greater China and began its evolution into a group. In 2009, Wah Tat entered the financial sector, establishing and investing in Heshan Guomin Microcredit Co., Ltd. In 2013, the group launched Prosperity International Finance, an overseas financial arm that further expanded its financing platform. That same year, Wah Tat Group was formally established, reorganising its real estate development, commercial management, property investment, financial investment and industrial trading businesses under one unified structure.

2009 Entered the financial sector 2013 Prosperity International Finance 2013 Wah Tat Group established
