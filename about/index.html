Corporate Overview

Rooted in Hong Kong, Reaching the World

Founded in 1980, Wah Tat Industrial (Group) Company Limited ("Wah Tat Group") is a Hong Kong-based, cross-industry conglomerate. Through more than three decades of rapid, steady growth, Wah Tat Group's operations now span the globe, with Hong Kong, Mainland China and the United States as its principal markets. Member companies of Wah Tat Group include Wah Tat Industrial (Far East) Limited, Wah Kei Real Estate Development Limited, Wah Tat Property Development Limited and Yihui Trading Management Limited, with businesses spanning real estate development, commercial management, property investment, financial investment and industrial trading. Guided by a forward-looking vision, Wah Tat Group has embraced the market's growing demand for health and sustainability, venturing into new frontiers such as the health industry and e-commerce to meet the challenges of the new era.

Upholding the core values of "striving for excellence and breakthrough innovation", Wah Tat Group is committed to delivering premium, efficient products and services through the all-round enhancement of its corporate competitiveness. Rooted in Hong Kong, facing the world, Wah Tat Group stands by the principle of "taking from society and giving back to society", steadfastly supporting charitable causes and contributing to the communities we serve.
