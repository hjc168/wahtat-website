Sustainability & Innovation

It's In Our DNA

Throughout the years, Wah Tat has developed a proud legacy of bringing new materials and production methods to revolutionize the way artificial floral and seasonal home decorations are manufactured. Innovation is at the heart of everything we do. Now, we are including sustainability alongside innovation as part of our ethos and DNA.
